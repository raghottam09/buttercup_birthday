My Dearest Buttercup, My Lady Love,

Happy Birthday, my love.

On my birthday, you wrote that we found each other when neither was searching, and that somehow, in the most effortless way, we became home to each other. You thanked me for holding you in a gentle way, and for never making you feel like you were 'too much'.

Today, on your birthday, I want to give you that truth right back: You could never be too much for the heart that was created to hold you.

You came into my life quietly, but you rewrote the meaning of peace. We have had our odd days, our distance, and moments that tested our patience across 2,500 kilometers. But through every single disagreement, my soul has never wavered for a fraction of a second. I will never leave your side. We don't fight to step back; we fight to hold on tighter.

You are not just my girlfriend. In my heart, in front of my family, and before every single person who knows my name, you are my future wife. Bangalore is eagerly counting down the days until your bags are unpacked here, your laughter fills our rooms, and long distance becomes just an old story we tell our kids.
